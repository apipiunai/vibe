Go-To-Market Strategy

3 phases · Digital-first · Partnership-driven

Phase 1 — Months 0–6
Bordeaux Pilot
  • 5 supermarket partners (Monoprix, Carrefour City)
  • 10 independent wine shops in Bordeaux
  • Partnership with local business schools (visibility & early users)
  • Social media launch: TikTok, Instagram
Phase 2 — Months 6–18
Paris & Toulouse
  • Scale to Paris & Toulouse: 2 major cities
  • National franchise deals (Carrefour, Leclerc, Nicolas)
  • KPI: 8,000 active users · 50 partners
Phase 3 — Months 18–36
National Scale
  • Full national rollout: all major cities
  • B2B widget for restaurants & hotels
  • Cocktails / other drinks versions
